# 🚗 Classic Models: Sales, Profitability & Market Basket Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and cross-selling opportunities for the **Classic Models** dataset. Using Microsoft Excel, I explored transactional data to identify revenue trends, evaluate product profitability, segment customers by credit limits, and uncover product relationships that can support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Evaluate sales performance across countries and markets.
* Identify the most profitable regions and product lines.
* Analyze customer spending based on credit limit tiers.
* Discover product combinations for cross-selling opportunities.
* Provide actionable recommendations to improve revenue and profitability.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### 1. Sales & Profitability by Country

* The **United States** generated the highest total revenue, contributing over **$1.52 million** in sales.
* Smaller markets such as **Austria (43.4%)** and the **Philippines (42.2%)** achieved the highest profit margins despite lower sales volumes.
* **Insight:** High-margin markets present attractive opportunities for expansion while operational improvements can increase profitability in high-revenue markets.

### 2. Market Basket Analysis

* Customers purchasing **Classic Cars** frequently also purchased **Vintage Cars (30.7%)** and **Trucks & Buses (20.8%)**.
* Product categories such as **Trains** and **Ships** showed relatively weak cross-selling relationships, indicating potential opportunities for promotional bundles.

### 3. Customer Credit Analysis

* Customers with credit limits exceeding **$150,000** generated the highest total sales and the greatest average order value (approximately **$37.8k**).
* Higher credit limits were generally associated with stronger purchasing activity.

## 🚀 Recommendations

1. Introduce bundled promotions featuring **Classic Cars** with **Vintage Cars** and **Trucks & Buses** to increase average order value.
2. Expand marketing efforts in high-margin countries such as Austria, the Philippines, and Japan.
3. Reward high-performing mid-tier customers with increased credit limits to encourage greater spending.
4. Improve promotional strategies for underperforming product categories to strengthen cross-selling opportunities.
